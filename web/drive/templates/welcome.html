This website opens the same encrypted files as the Stratus terminal.

Your identity is your Linux device, so Drive is opened from the terminal:

  1. Run ./stratus.sh terminal and join your server.
  2. Choose Browser Access once, to pick a browser passphrase.
  3. Open Hosted Applications, select stratus-drive and press Open in Browser.
